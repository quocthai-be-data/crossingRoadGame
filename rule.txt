CROSSING ROAD GAME - RULES

1. Use W/A/S/D to move your character (Y).
2. Cross all lanes to reach the finish line (===).
3. Avoid vehicles (C = Car, T = Truck) and
   animals (B = Bird, D = Dinosaur).
4. Cars move fast (speed 2), trucks are slow (1).
5. Traffic lights (G/R) control vehicles only,
   animals move freely.
6. Each level has more obstacles.
7. You start with 10 points per level.
   Each death costs 1 point.
8. Reach 0 points = Game Over.
9. Complete all 5 levels to win!

CONTROLS:
  W - Move up       A - Move left
  S - Move down     D - Move right
  P - Pause         L - Save game
  R - Records       ESC - Back to menu
